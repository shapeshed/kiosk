Kiosk is a Hacker News reader for Android.

It is built around reading, not browsing. Browse Hacker News feeds, search historical stories, read articles in a distraction-free native reader, and keep comments close at hand.

Features:
- Browse Top, New, Best, Ask, Show, and Jobs.
- Search historical Hacker News stories.
- Read articles in a native reader powered by Mozilla Readability.
- Swipe between stories in the current feed.
- Open comments quickly in a bottom sheet.
- Use reader themes, fonts, Speed Reader, and Read Aloud.
- Expand article images and pinch to zoom.
- Open PDFs, X/Twitter, YouTube, and external links in the default app or browser.

Kiosk has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking SDKs, Firebase, Crashlytics, or Google Play Services dependency.
